RockTrack demo songs
====================

Chiptune arrangements for RockTrack, written as text and generated by
make_demos.py (Python 3, standard library only):

    python3 make_demos.py [OUTDIR]        write the .xm files and greensleeves.mid
    python3 make_demos.py --check [DIR]   re-read the .xm files and check them

Every instrument is a RockTrack synth patch (sample names "rt1:...", ADSR
volume envelopes), so RockTrack shows and edits the sounds. The songs also
play in MilkyTracker, OpenMPT, libxmp and other XM players.

Licence: the compositions below are in the public domain. Every
arrangement is by RockTrack contributors and dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
"Sparkstone", "Quartz Comet", "Cinder Warden" and "Lemon Kite" are
original tracks by RockTrack contributors, also CC0 1.0, as is
make_demos.py itself.

The other chiptunes in this folder (drozerix_*, ant512_*, djego_flochs_*,
m4kachu_*) are by their composers under their own licences; see
THIRD_PARTY.txt.


ode_to_joy.xm   Ode to Joy
    Ludwig van Beethoven, Symphony No. 9 in D minor, Op. 125, finale (1824).
    Public domain. Arrangement by RockTrack contributors, CC0 1.0.
    The theme (16 bars, the familiar unsyncopated form) three times:
    quietly, with drums and echo, then its second half in thirds with a
    ritardando. 6 channels, 1:29.

fur_elise.xm    Fur Elise (Bagatelle No. 25 in A minor)
    Ludwig van Beethoven, WoO 59 (1810). Public domain.
    Arrangement by RockTrack contributors, CC0 1.0.
    The A section (bars 1-8 with the first ending) four times: as written,
    with an echo, as a music box over a soft pad, and a last time ending on
    the closing A. The episodes are not included. 4 channels, 0:48.

canon_in_d.xm   Canon in D
    Johann Pachelbel (c. 1680-1706). Public domain.
    Arrangement by RockTrack contributors, CC0 1.0.
    The ground bass and the first three violin variations as a three-voice
    canon, then the same canon again as a chiptune with drums.
    8 channels, 1:09.

mountain_king.xm   In the Hall of the Mountain King
    Edvard Grieg, Peer Gynt, Op. 23 (1875). Public domain.
    Arrangement by RockTrack contributors, CC0 1.0.
    The main B minor phrase, sixteen times, rising through the octaves and
    accelerating from 92 to 248 BPM, then closing chords. The answering
    phrase in F sharp is not included. 6 channels, 1:00.

gymnopedie.xm   Gymnopedie No. 1
    Erik Satie (1888). Public domain.
    Arrangement by RockTrack contributors, CC0 1.0.
    The opening: the left-hand introduction and the first melodic phrase
    (bars 1-12), twice, with a short left-hand coda. 6 channels, 1:12.

bach_prelude.xm   Prelude in C major, BWV 846
    Johann Sebastian Bach, The Well-Tempered Clavier, Book I (1722).
    Public domain. Arrangement by RockTrack contributors, CC0 1.0.
    Bars 1-31 and the final C major chord (the coda bars 32-34 are left
    out), with an echo on the broken chords. 4 channels, 1:52.

greensleeves.xm   Greensleeves
    Traditional English, 16th century. Public domain.
    Arrangement by RockTrack contributors, CC0 1.0.
    Verse and refrain with lute and tabor, the refrain again with a viol
    harmony and chip arpeggios. 6 channels, 1:42.

greensleeves.mid   Greensleeves (MIDI)
    The same arrangement as a Standard MIDI File (type 1: a tempo track and
    one track per voice with General MIDI programs, drums on channel 10),
    for RockTrack's MIDI import. Arrangement by RockTrack contributors,
    CC0 1.0.

sparkstone.xm   Sparkstone
    Original track by RockTrack contributors (2026), CC0 1.0.
    An upbeat chiptune in A major showing the tracker off: 0xy arpeggio
    chords, 3xx glides, 4xy vibrato, synth drums with retrigger rolls, an
    echo channel one row behind the lead, a harmony line and a riser.
    Loops back to the first verse. 8 channels, 1:46.

quartz_comet.xm   Quartz Comet
    Original track by RockTrack contributors (2026), CC0 1.0.
    A fast, driving chiptune in E minor at 160 BPM: a pedal-note riff, a
    verse and a long-note chorus over octave and galloping basses, a solo
    with a ping-pong echo and a pitch dive, a breakdown with a tremolo pad
    and glides, a build that steps up from B to C sharp with a noise
    whoosh, and the last chorus a tone higher in F sharp minor with a
    harmony line. Ends on a final hit. 8 channels, 1:38.

cinder_warden.xm   Cinder Warden
    Original track by RockTrack contributors (2026), CC0 1.0.
    A dark "final boss" theme in C minor: a funeral bell and timpani
    rolls, a chromatic bass ostinato under power-chord stabs, tremolo
    strings, a lament breakdown, and a build that speeds up from 138 to
    146 BPM into a second phase a semitone higher, in C sharp minor.
    Ends on a slowing final hit. 8 channels, 1:42.

lemon_kite.xm   Lemon Kite
    Original track by RockTrack contributors (2026), CC0 1.0.
    A sunny swing tune in G major on a triplet grid (three rows to the
    beat): a square and a flute trade phrases, the chorus goes in thirds
    over off-beat chord stabs, then a music-box breakdown, a triplet snare
    build with a rising kite whistle, and the last chorus in A major with
    a ping-pong echo and a glockenspiel. 8 channels, 1:48.
