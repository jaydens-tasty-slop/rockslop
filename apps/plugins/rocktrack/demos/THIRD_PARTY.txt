Third-party demo songs
======================

These modules are by other people and are shipped unmodified, exactly as
downloaded from The Mod Archive (the MD5 sums match the archive's pages).
Every licence below was set by the composer for that module and is shown
on the module's page under "License". The composers' own credits are in
the instrument and sample names inside each file; keep the files
unmodified so those credits stay with them.

If a song is changed and saved, the result is a derivative work: keep the
credit, and for CC BY say what was changed.

The full CC BY 4.0 text is LICENSE.CC-BY-4.0.txt next to this file (in
.rockbox/licenses/apps/rocktrack/ on the card).
