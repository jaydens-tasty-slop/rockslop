RockGPT model files (.llm)
==========================

The model files RockGPT loads (rockgpt.llm, rockgpt-draft.llm) were made as
follows.

1. Pretraining: a small llama2-style transformer was trained from scratch
   with Andrej Karpathy's llama2.c (https://github.com/karpathy/llama2.c,
   MIT) on the TinyStories dataset (Eldan and Li, Microsoft Research;
   Community Data License Agreement - Sharing, Version 1.0, CDLA-Sharing-1.0),
   tokenized with a 512-token sentencepiece vocabulary.

2. Fine-tuning: the model was then fine-tuned on short persona chats, mixed
   with TinyStories, that were generated locally with Qwen3.5 9B (Alibaba
   Cloud, Apache-2.0).

3. The checkpoints were exported to the int8 .llm format that llm.c reads
   (tools/rockgpt/export.py in the YP3 repository). The small draft model
   was distilled from the main one.
