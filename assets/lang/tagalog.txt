THIS CAN BRICK YOUR PLAYER
= PUWEDENG MA-BRICK ANG PLAYER MO
Rockbox on this player is unofficial and experimental. A flash that goes wrong (a loose cable, a crash, a bug of ours) can leave the player dead, and there may be no way to bring it back. rockslop keeps a backup of the stock firmware, but a backup can't save every player. The risk is yours alone.
= Hindi opisyal at eksperimental ang Rockbox sa player na ito. Kapag pumalpak ang flash (maluwag na cable, crash, bug namin), puwedeng mamatay ang player, at baka wala nang paraan para buhayin ito. May backup ang rockslop ng stock firmware, pero hindi kayang iligtas ng backup ang bawat player. Sa iyo lang ang panganib.
YOUR WARRANTY IS VOID
= WALA NANG BISA ANG WARRANTY MO
Flashing firmware the maker didn't write voids the warranty. Whatever happens to this player from now on is yours to deal with: no repair, no replacement, no refund. Going back to stock doesn't change that.
= Nawawalan ng bisa ang warranty kapag nag-flash ka ng firmware na hindi gawa ng manufacturer. Anuman ang mangyari sa player na ito mula ngayon, ikaw na ang bahala: walang repair, walang kapalit, walang refund. Hindi iyon mababago ng pagbalik sa stock.
DON'T TAKE IT BACK
= HUWAG ITONG IBALIK
Never return a flashed player, claim on it, or complain to the seller or the maker about it. Rockbox runs on these players because nobody has locked them down. If flashed players start coming back, the next batch will be locked, and that's the end of Rockbox on them for everyone. If yours breaks, bring it to rockslop, never to the shop.
= Huwag kailanman ibalik ang na-flash na player, mag-claim dito, o magreklamo tungkol dito sa seller o sa manufacturer. Tumatakbo ang Rockbox sa mga player na ito dahil walang nag-lock sa kanila. Kapag nagsimulang bumalik ang mga na-flash na player, naka-lock na ang susunod na batch, at iyon na ang katapusan ng Rockbox sa kanila para sa lahat. Kapag nasira ang sa iyo, dalhin ito sa rockslop, hindi kailanman sa tindahan.
