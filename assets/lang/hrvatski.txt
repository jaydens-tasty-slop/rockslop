THIS CAN BRICK YOUR PLAYER
= OVO MOŽE UNIŠTITI TVOJ UREĐAJ
Rockbox on this player is unofficial and experimental. A flash that goes wrong (a loose cable, a crash, a bug of ours) can leave the player dead, and there may be no way to bring it back. rockslop keeps a backup of the stock firmware, but a backup can't save every player. The risk is yours alone.
= Rockbox na ovom uređaju neslužben je i eksperimentalan. Ako flashanje pođe po zlu (labav kabel, rušenje, greška u našem kodu), uređaj može ostati mrtav i možda ga više neće biti moguće oživjeti. rockslop čuva sigurnosnu kopiju tvorničkog firmwarea, ali kopija ne može spasiti svaki uređaj. Rizik je samo tvoj.
YOUR WARRANTY IS VOID
= JAMSTVO VIŠE NE VRIJEDI
Flashing firmware the maker didn't write voids the warranty. Whatever happens to this player from now on is yours to deal with: no repair, no replacement, no refund. Going back to stock doesn't change that.
= Flashanje firmwarea koji nije napisao proizvođač poništava jamstvo. Što god se od sada dogodi ovom uređaju, tvoja je briga: nema popravka, nema zamjene, nema povrata novca. Vraćanje na tvornički firmware to ne mijenja.
DON'T TAKE IT BACK
= NE VRAĆAJ GA
Never return a flashed player, claim on it, or complain to the seller or the maker about it. Rockbox runs on these players because nobody has locked them down. If flashed players start coming back, the next batch will be locked, and that's the end of Rockbox on them for everyone. If yours breaks, bring it to rockslop, never to the shop.
= Nikad ne vraćaj flashani uređaj, ne reklamiraj ga i ne žali se zbog njega prodavaču ni proizvođaču. Rockbox radi na ovim uređajima jer ih nitko nije zaključao. Ako se flashani uređaji počnu vraćati, sljedeća serija bit će zaključana i to je kraj Rockboxa na njima za sve. Ako se tvoj pokvari, obrati se rockslopu, nikad trgovini.
