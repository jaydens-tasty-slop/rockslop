THIS CAN BRICK YOUR PLAYER
= ÇOULA POUT TOUWER VOSSE APAREY
Rockbox on this player is unofficial and experimental. A flash that goes wrong (a loose cable, a crash, a bug of ours) can leave the player dead, and there may be no way to bring it back. rockslop keeps a backup of the stock firmware, but a backup can't save every player. The risk is yours alone.
= Rockbox so cist aparey ci n' est nén ofiçiel, c' est co esperimintå. On flash ki toûne mal (on cåbe ki n' tént nén bén, on crash, ene aroke da nosse) pout touwer l' aparey, et i gn a ptete pus nou moyén del fé rivni. rockslop wåde ene schape do firmware d' oridjine, mins ene schape ni pout nén sôver tos les apareys. Li risse, c' est vos tot seu ki l' prind.
YOUR WARRANTY IS VOID
= VOSSE GARANTEYE EST PERDOWE
Flashing firmware the maker didn't write voids the warranty. Whatever happens to this player from now on is yours to deal with: no repair, no replacement, no refund. Going back to stock doesn't change that.
= Flashî on firmware ki l' fabricant n' a nén scrit fwait piede li garanteye. Tot çou ki va ariver a cist aparey ci a pårti d' asteure, c' est vosse afwaire: nou rapontiaedje, nou ôte aparey, nou rindaedje des cwårs. Rivni å firmware d' oridjine ni candje rén a ça.
DON'T TAKE IT BACK
= NEL RAPORTEZ NÉN
